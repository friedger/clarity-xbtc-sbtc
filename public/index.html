🔄 xBTC to sBTC Swap

Swap your xBTC tokens for sBTC at a 1:1 ratio

  1. Deposit xBTC into the contract and receive swapping xBTC (SWXBTC).
  2. xBTC is converted to sBTC by the custodian.
  3. Claim your sBTC by burning swapping xBTC (SWXBTC).

ℹ️ Important Information

  • The swap ratio is 1:1 (1 xBTC = 1 sBTC)
  • The swap is a two step process: first you deposit xBTC to mint SWXBTC, once, xBTC have been unwrapped by custodian, then you claim sBTC by burning SWXBTC
  • Make sure you have sufficient xBTC balance
  • Transaction fees apply (paid in STX)
